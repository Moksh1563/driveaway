# Surface brief: Driveaway site (home first; fleet, car detail, booking, auth, dashboard, admin inherit)

Scope and mode: public site is Persuade (home, fleet, car detail); booking, auth, dashboard and admin are Operate surfaces inside the same world, where the world shows as precision and restraint.
Audience: luxury and exotic renters in Ahmedabad. Job: find a car, pick hub and dates, book. Proof: the real fleet (18 cars, rates, ratings). Constraint: keep every feature, the name, the car-icon logo, the dark/light toggle. No new invented claims.
Memorable moment: picking a swatch chip repaints the whole hero in that factory paint and swaps the car.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Choose a colour, get the car. Driveaway is a paint-shop swatch book; every category owns a factory paint and the page wears the paint you pick. Refuses the exotic-rental default of a dark photo hero, gold button and equal card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Primer ground #ECEAE6 (light) and clear-coat #0C0C0D (dark); all chrome neutral ink. Paints: Hypercar French Racing Blue #1F3C88, Supercar Rosso Corsa #D0021B, Luxury Racing Green #0F4D2E, SUV Giallo #F2B705, Electric Voltage Teal #0E9AAA, Sports Arancio #E8590C, Sedan Graphite #4A4E57, Hatchback Lilac #7B61C9. Lacquer chips with a clear-coat highlight sweep; unavailable cars as matte unlit chips. Archivo: 125% width black for display, 62-75% for paint codes and labels, 100% for body. Ink pill buttons.

STORY: A visitor sees a supercar drenched in its paint, learns the fleet is sorted by paint, fills hub and dates on the order slip, and books.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed paint field (default Supercar). Top-left headline ~120px expanded uppercase "Supercars, in Rosso Corsa."; paint-code line under it. Car photo right in a large chip frame with highlight. Order slip left (hub, category, from, to, primary CTA). Swatch fan on the bottom edge: choosing a chip repaints the field, headline and car (signature interaction, ~500ms colour wipe; reduced motion swaps instantly).

FORM: grounded candidate 4 of 7 (automotive paint-swatch book), seed:24b0e5d8743090e9. Raises: unlit chips for unavailable cars (seven-segment); all chroma spent on paint (civic bureau); specs on leader lines (tensegrity); price span in view on fleet head rule (lexicon); one axis paint to car to booking (Versailles); dashboard garage ring of booked paints (Cyworld).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
